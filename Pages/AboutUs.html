About SpeedyRent

Your trusted partner for all your car rental needs since 2010

Our Story

SpeedyRent was founded in 2010 with a simple mission: to provide high-quality rental cars at affordable prices with exceptional customer service. What started as a small fleet of just 5 cars has now grown to over 500 vehicles across 20 locations nationwide.

Our journey began when our founder, John Smith, experienced frustration with existing car rental services. He envisioned a more customer-friendly approach that eliminated hidden fees and complicated contracts. Today, we're proud to be one of the fastest-growing car rental companies in the country.

Our Mission

At SpeedyRent, our mission is to make car rental simple, transparent, and enjoyable. We believe in:

  • Providing well-maintained, reliable vehicles
  • Offering transparent pricing with no hidden fees
  • Delivering exceptional customer service at every touchpoint
  • Embracing technology to streamline the rental process
  • Reducing our environmental impact through fuel-efficient and electric vehicles
